1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (THE CORE IDEA IN PLAIN TERMS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every Indian city operates hundreds of public transport buses traversing arterial 
roads, ring roads, and congested corridors daily. Currently, these buses are 
treated merely as "tracked assets" (showing where the bus is on a transit schedule).

Meanwhile, smart cities deploy municipal Command & Control Centers (like BEL's 
flagship EQUINOX platform). However, these systems rely on FIXED CCTV cameras 
mounted on intersection poles. Between intersections, the city has massive blind 
spots: kilometers of road with undetected potholes, hazardous waterlogging in 
underpasses, broken street signs, and traffic chokepoints.

OUR IDEA:
We turn existing public transit buses into intelligent, mobile sensing probes. 
By mounting low-cost cameras and depth sensors on buses, edge AI detects road 
hazards in real time. 

CRUCIAL TECHNICAL BREAKTHROUGH:
If 500 buses pass through a city, they generate thousands of noisy, redundant, 
and weather-corrupted camera detections. If you dump raw detections into a city 
dashboard, operators are overwhelmed with false alarms.

We built an EVIDENCE FUSION ENGINE that:
1. Discounts correlated errors (e.g., when rain coats bus lenses, multiple buses 
   see puddles that look like potholes; our math discounts redundant passes).
2. Evaluates operational municipal consequence (a moderate pothole causing an 
   11-minute bus route delay near a school outranks a severe pothole on an empty 
   suburban road).
3. Ingests structured, verified alerts directly into BEL EQUINOX's Context 
   Broker, triggering automated municipal repair work orders (SOPs).

3. THE TECHNICAL PROBLEM: WHY SIMPLE "YOLO + BUS" FAILS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most teams believe the challenge is running YOLO on a bus windshield. It is not.
The hard problems are:

A. THE CORRELATED NOISE PROBLEM:
   Bus 1 sees a pothole at 2:00 PM (heavy rain, water glare).
   Bus 2 sees a pothole at 2:05 PM (heavy rain, water glare).
   Bus 3 sees a pothole at 2:10 PM (heavy rain, water glare).
   Naive systems treat this as "3 independent confirmations = 99.9% confidence."
   In reality, all 3 cameras suffer from the same optical failure mode. 
   Our engine calculates the environmental correlation coefficient (rho=0.72) 
   and discounts duplicate confidence:
   N_effective = N / [1 + (N - 1) * rho]
   7 raw passes in rain = only 4.2 effective independent observations.

B. DETECTION CONFIDENCE != MUNICIPAL PRIORITY (THE 5-SECOND DEMO MOMENT):
   Consider two road segments:
   - Segment A (Outer Ring Road suburban): 1 bus pass, 97% camera confidence, 
     zero traffic delay, zero pedestrian risk.
   - Segment B (Connaught Place radial): 7 bus passes, 84% camera confidence, 
     +11.4 min transit choke, extreme pedestrian density (school/market).
   Naive detector ranking: Segment A > Segment B (because 97% > 84%).
   Equinox Fusion ranking: Segment B > Segment A (because municipal harm is huge).
